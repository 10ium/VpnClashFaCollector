ANIME QUALITY STANDARD
Every frame must look as if it was drawn by professional anime animators and painted by master background artists for a finished theatrical film.

Line art: Confident, delicate, hand-inked anime line work with subtle variation in thickness and pressure.
Coloring: Traditional anime cel coloring with clean color areas, simple hard-edged shadows, and a soft, natural palette.
Backgrounds: Richly detailed, hand-painted anime backgrounds in a gouache style, with visible brushwork, natural color variation, layered foliage, and real depth.
Lighting: Golden sunrise light, colorful shadows, glowing mist, and soft atmosphere that unite the robot and background.
Animation: Authentic hand-drawn anime timing with real weight, strong key poses, natural holds, and overlapping movement in vines, grass, leaves, birds, and falling debris. The robot should rise slowly and feel massive, then launch with explosive power.
Direction: Cinematic anime shots, beautiful composition, thoughtful staging, and pacing that starts quietly, builds tension, and ends in a soaring climax.
IT MUST NOT LOOK LIKE
- 3D: No plastic materials, CGI shading, visible polygons, game-engine lighting, or computer-perfect perspective.

You are free to change any part of the prompt, if it would result, in better results.
- AI art: No warped shapes, melted details, flickering, or changing robot designs.
- Non-anime art: No Western cartoon designs, realistic rendering, soft painterly shading on the robot, or generic digital illustration.
- Amateur work: No stiff poses, empty backgrounds, repeated trees, muddy colors, clip art, placeholders, or unfinished details.
