Aether v1.3.0
Aether is a user-space proxy client. It tunnels traffic out of a filtered network and exposes a local SOCKS5 proxy on 
[127.0.0.1:1819](http://127.0.0.1:1819/)
. Point a browser, terminal, or system proxy at that address and your traffic leaves through the tunnel.
